Contact & Support

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding Pixel Chronicles — or if you want to delete your account for any reason — you can reach us anytime by email at support@moonforge-games.com.

We will do our best to respond promptly and assist you.

Deleting your account or data

Email us from the address linked to your account, or include your in-game player name. See the Privacy Policy for what happens to your data once you ask.

Reporting a bug

Telling us your player name, roughly when it happened, and what you were doing at the time makes a report much faster to act on.
