Contact & Support

Nearly everything happens on the Discord. It is the fastest way to reach us — bug reports, questions, suggestions, and update announcements all live there, and other players are usually around to help too.

Community & supportJoin the Discord

🐛 Reporting a bug

Post it in the Discord. Telling us your player name, roughly when it happened, and what you were doing at the time makes a report much faster to act on — a screenshot helps even more.

💡 Ideas and feedback

Also the Discord. Pixel Chronicles is made by one person, so what the community pushes for genuinely shapes what gets built next.

🔒 Account or data requests

For anything involving your account or personal data — including deleting it — email support@moonforge-games.com instead. Those requests need a written record, so please do not send them over Discord.

Email from the address linked to your account, or include your in-game player name. The Privacy Policy explains what happens to your data once you ask.

We will do our best to respond promptly and assist you.
